3. **Yerel Depolama:**
   - Kredi bakiyesi, günlük görevler, tank görünümleri, oyun ayarları ve özel haritalar yalnızca cihazınızda (SharedPreferences) saklanır.

## English

**Tank 90** respects your privacy. Data processed by the app is used solely to provide gameplay, online matchmaking, and advertising:

1. **Online Multiplayer & Leaderboard (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):**
   - An **anonymous user identifier (Anonymous UID)** is generated automatically to support online Co-Op/1v1 rooms and the leaderboard.
   - Your chosen in-game nickname, rank, win count, and high score may be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore.
   - No real name, phone number, or password is required.

2. **Advertising (Google AdMob):**
   - Tank 90 uses **Google AdMob** to display banner and rewarded ads.
   - Google AdMob may collect device/advertising identifiers (Advertising ID), approximate location, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention.
   - Learn more about Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. **Local Storage:**
   - Credits, daily quests, unlocked skins, settings, and custom maps are stored locally on your device.

4. **Security & Contact:**
   - Data transmitted to cloud services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
   - For questions or data deletion requests, contact: **burhanaydin2855@gmail.com**
